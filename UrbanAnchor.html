Questions

Fair things to ask

Try it. Ask one what a fair rent is in your city and you'll get a range so wide it's useless, because it doesn't know your commute, your office address, your start date or what you can put down up front.

A blueprint knows all four, and that changes the answers completely. It tells you that a security deposit above one month is illegal in New York and quietly standard in Pennsylvania. That a broker fee can add $3,000 in one city and doesn't exist in the next. That the 40× income rule will disqualify you before you've seen a single apartment, and exactly what to do about it. Those aren't facts you'd know to ask for — which is the whole problem with asking.

Then it's built to be used, not read: a dated timeline, tickable checkboxes that save in the file, budget options you click, and the admin steps in the order that actually works, so you don't discover in week three that one of them needed doing first.

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Short version: we collect what we need to build your relocation blueprint, we use it to build and deliver that blueprint, and we don't sell it to anyone. The longer version is below.

What we collect

When you complete the survey, we collect what you type into it:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number, so we can send you your blueprint and reach you about it.
  • Move details — your destination city, employer or office location, target move date, and the concern you describe.
  • Preferences — commute method and tolerance, rent budget, living arrangement, and what you want from a neighborhood.
  • Technical context — browser type, language, time zone, screen size and referring page, which we use to debug problems and understand which channels bring people here.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, your government ID number, bank details, or immigration documents. Payment card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.

How we use it

  • To generate your personalized blueprint, which involves sending your survey answers to our AI provider for processing.
  • To email you that blueprint and any follow-up about your order.
  • To answer you when you contact support.
  • To improve the product in aggregate — for example, noticing that a city we cover has thin neighborhood data.

We do not use your answers to train AI models, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of processors to run the service. Each one only receives what it needs:

  • Our AI provider — receives your survey answers to generate the blueprint text.
  • Our email provider — receives your name, email address and the blueprint file in order to deliver it.
  • Stripe — handles payment and receives your payment details directly. We only see the outcome and the last four digits.
  • Our hosting provider — stores the data at rest.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

How long we keep it

Survey responses and generated blueprints are kept for 24 months so you can request a copy if you lose the file. Payment records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

Your choices

Wherever you live, we will honour these requests:

  • Access — ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix anything wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your data, subject to records we must legally keep.
  • Objection — tell us to stop processing your data for a given purpose.

If you're in California, the CCPA gives you these rights explicitly, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. If you're in the EEA or UK, the GDPR does the same. Email us and we'll action it within 30 days.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your survey answers live in the page while you fill it in and are sent to us only when you press submit.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Children

UrbanAnchor is intended for adults relocating for work or study. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll update the date at the top and email anyone with an active order.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests, or anything else: support@urbananchor.com.

This policy is provided as a working starting point and is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it against your actual data practices and the jurisdictions you operate in before you launch.
